Open and edit XLSX, XLS, CSV and TSV files in project and Assistant workspaces. Cell edits preserve existing XLSX formatting, leading zeros in delimited text identifiers, and hidden worksheet states in XLS files. Workbooks containing XLS formulas open read-only. Workspace file editing requires Xpert contracts and plugin SDK 3.39.0 or later in the 3.x series.
