Add a native OfficeCLI Workbench and assistant plugin for creating, importing, previewing, editing, validating, versioning, downloading, and deleting DOCX, XLSX, and PPTX files stored in Xpert Workspace Files.

Support direct visual edits and AI-assisted OfficeCLI commands, professional Word styles and tables of contents, bounded version retention, persistent checksum-verified OfficeCLI runtimes, startup prewarming, and actionable Chinese preview errors.
