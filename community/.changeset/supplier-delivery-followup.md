Add the supplier delivery follow-up app: buyers paste a supplier's delivery reply into a workbench, the assistant matches it to order lines and proposes evidence-backed date changes through five middleware tools, and order dates change only when a person confirms, with a change log and timeline. Includes the assistant template, a remote workbench view, and needs-follow-up, timeout and stale-proposal recovery.
