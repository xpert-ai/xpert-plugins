The Valve Business Workbench turns published valve ontology snapshots into an operational engineering surface. Users can browse objects, inspect relationships and evidence, review constraints, ask the Assistant for an analysis, and govern Action proposals through preflight, human approval, Demo execution, and audit.

Normal Workbench and Assistant operations are read-only toward ontology data. The only ontology write path is the explicit, user-confirmed initializer for the plugin-owned `valve-engineering-ontology` bundle. Proposal and decision audit records remain isolated in the plugin namespace.
