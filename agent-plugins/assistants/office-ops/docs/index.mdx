---
title: Office Ops
description: An office operations Assistant with durable ledgers and reviewed external actions.
---

Office Ops ships seven skills and an Assistant template. Import it in Web or Desktop, choose a workspace and model, then finish the language, timezone and office-site setup.

Local ledgers are isolated per user. The template initializes only missing files. Reinstalling never overwrites existing business data. Three routines remain paused until enabled.

See [Workflows and verification](/usage).
